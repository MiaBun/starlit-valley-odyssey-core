# Lunchbox

The lunchbox is your go-to solution for food storage!

they come in tiers, each decreasing the cooldown between feeding and increasing storage slots!

right click on them to put food inside of the lunchbox.

the tiers are as follows:

| Tier                  | Slots | Cooldown |
|-----------------------|-------|----------|
| Lunchbox              | 3     | 60       |
| Deluxe Lunchbox       | 4     | 40       |
| Super Deluxe Lunchbox | 5     | 30       |
| Premium Lunchbox      | 6     | 20       |
| Ultimate Lunchbox     | 8     | 15       |
| Collectors Lunchbox   | 9     | 5        |
