# Toolbelt

The toolbelt is your go-to solution for tool storage!

they come in tiers, each increasing storage slots!

right click on them to put tools inside of the toolbelt and configure the hotbar slot.

then equip it in your Curio slot.

then when you select the confgiured hotbar slot and press left alt you'll enter a radial menu where you can quickly take out your stored tools!

the tiers are as follows:

| Tier                  | Slots |
|-----------------------|-------|
| Toolbelt              | 3     |
| Deluxe Toolbelt       | 4     |
| Super Deluxe Toolbelt | 5     |
| Premium Toolbelt      | 6     |
| Ultimate Toolbelt     | 8     |
| Collectors Toolbelt   | 9     |

*please note that upgrading toolbelts does not carry over it's contents, removing contents before upgrading is adviced.*
