# Charcoal Kiln

The Charcoal Kiln is the primary way of obtaining Coal, Whilst you can still find Coal Ore Veins in the world, These have been heavily nerfed to be rarer, 

You might be wondering, Why not just use Logs and other Wooden Items as Fuel for Furnaces?

We removed Logs and other Wooden Items as Fuel for Furnaces to incentivise using the Charcoal Kiln.

So how do i use the Charcoal Kiln?

You put Logs into the slot to the left, wait 20 seconds and it'll give you Coal in the slot on the right for you to use!
